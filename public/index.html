The approach

Hair that is
maintained,
not fixed

Extensions are not a one-off. A set is fitted, then kept — moved up as your own hair grows, re-blended as your colour shifts, and taken down cleanly when it is time.

Every fitting is hand-placed in a private studio, colour-matched to you and cut in so the join never announces itself.

The process

Consult
Fit
Maintain

1/

Consult

A free thirty minutes. We look at the condition of your own hair, match your colour properly and decide which method suits it — then order the right hair in advance.

2/

Fit

Hand-placed in a private studio. No heat and no glue against your own hair, whichever method you go for. Cut and blended before you leave.

3/

Maintain

Back every six to eight weeks. The bonds move up as your hair grows, which keeps the set seamless and takes the tension off the hair underneath.

Maintenance

Recover
and protect

Left too long, extensions begin pulling on the hair they are attached to. Moving them up on schedule takes that tension off, guards against breakage and lets your own hair keep growing underneath.

Staying on top of appointments is also less work in the chair, and the results last longer.
